# The Refund Agent Challenge

You built a refund agent in the labs. Now it's your turn to take it further,
together with a partner. At the end, a jury picks the team with the best work,
and that team wins a special prize.

## How it works

1. **Team up.** Find a partner. You'll work as a team of two.
2. **Pick an idea.** What would make your refund agent better?
3. **Build it.** Use one of your two AWS accounts.
4. **Show it.** At the end, show the jury what you built.

## What to build

This is your chance to play with **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore** and see what it
can do. In the labs you followed the steps. Now you can explore.

AgentCore has many parts you can experiment with, for example:

- **Gateway**: give your agent new tools.
- **Identity**: control who can use the agent, and what the agent can reach.
- **Memory**: help your agent remember what matters.
- **Policy**: set rules your agent has to follow.
- **Runtime**: run your own agent code.
- **Observability** and **Evaluations**: see what your agent does, and how well.
- **Code Interpreter** and **Browser**: built-in tools your agent can use.

Try one part in depth, or mix a few together. Anything that makes the refund
agent better, safer or more useful is welcome. Ideas that go beyond what you
did in the labs are the most fun to see.

## Time

You have about **180 minutes**, demos included. When staff call time, please
stop building and get ready to show what you have.

## A few ground rules

- **Use any tools you like.** Claude Code, the AWS console, CloudShell or your
  own code are all fine.
- **Build in your own account**, in `us-east-1`, as in the labs.
- **Stick with Amazon Nova Pro** as the model. Other models may need extra setup
  in your account, which would take time away from building.
- **Use fake data only.** Please keep real money, real cards, real personal
  data and real API keys out of it.
- **Ask for help any time.** Staff are happy to help you when you're stuck.

## How the jury decides

There are no points. The jury watches every demo and thinks about:

- **The idea.** Would a real refund agent find it useful?
- **Does it work?** Showing it live is best.
- **Showing, not just telling.** Let us see it in action.
- **Understanding.** Can you both explain what you built and why?
- **Ambition.** Trying something hard counts, even if it isn't perfect.
- **The demo.** Clear and easy to follow.

If something only half works, that's fine. Just tell us which part works and
which part doesn't yet.

## What to deliver

**Must have:**

- **A live demo**, showing what you built.

**Nice to have:**

- **Next steps:** what you would add with more time.

Have fun, and good luck!
